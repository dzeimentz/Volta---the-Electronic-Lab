**Full description** (max. 4000):

Volta 1 is a virtual electronics experiment kit for children aged 8 to 12.

Put bulbs, LEDs, resistors, push buttons, motors and transistors on the base board, draw wires to the battery and switch on. What happens next is calculated by a real circuit simulation: bulbs glow brighter or dimmer, motors spin, buzzers beep – and if you build without a series resistor, you'll see an LED burn out. One tap on "Undo" and it's whole again.

RESEARCH LIKE IN A REAL LAB
• 61 experiments in five levels – from the first circuit to transistor switches
• Every experiment starts with a research question: guess, build, observe, understand
• Step-by-step tips and a build guide when you get stuck
• A free lab for your own ideas

TICK HELPS YOU
The mascot Tick explains how everything works, asks the research questions, gives tips and helps with troubleshooting: it looks at your build and asks questions about the spots it notices – you find the solution yourself.

MEASURE AND UNDERSTAND
• Multimeter for voltage, current and resistance
• Voltage displays on the contact fields, visible current flow
• A circuit diagram that grows as you build
• Recorder for voltages over time
• Toolbox with colour code, E series, parts glossary and formula calculator
• Modules such as blinker, tone and timer module – with a "Look inside" at their circuit

FOR REAL, TOO
Every experiment comes with a build card as a PDF with parts list and steps to rebuild it with real parts – only with batteries up to 9 volts, never at the mains socket.

FOR PARENTS AND SCHOOLS
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no data collection, no internet needed
• Parents' area protected by your own PIN
• Researcher profiles for siblings or a class – just a symbol and a nickname
• Teacher tasks via QR code or a code to type in
• Presentation mode for projectors and whiteboards
• Classroom sound: off or headphones only
• Managed configuration for school devices (MDM)
• Photos and notes in the research notebook – everything stays on the device
